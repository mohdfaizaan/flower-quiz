About Flowers of Quran

About our project:

Flowers of Quran is a part of 'Aayaatul Quran' Project and it combines a printed book with online learning.

We warmly welcome children to join us in learning and understanding the verses of the Quran.

We also invite volunteers to assist in delivering the book to school students, and we welcome sponsors and supporters to help us spread the message of the Quran.

Inspired by the Hadith, “Pass on from me, even if it is only one verse” (Sahih al-Bukhari: 3461). Let us all work together to convey the message of the Quran, especially to the next generation.

Competition Details

Thank you for your interest in joining the ‘Flowers of Quran’ Competition! 🌸

To participate, please complete the following steps:

  1. Register online for the ‘Flowers of Quran’ course.
  2. Study all 234 Flowers of Quran carefully.
  3. Complete the course and obtain your Certificate of Completion.
  4. Submit your request to join the competition through the competition portal.
  5. Be available online at the scheduled date and time of the competition.
Please note: The competition will feature any 10 flowers. Quranic phrases will be displayed alongside their English translations, and you will need to match each Quranic phrase with its correct meaning.
